# E-027 and E-028 — production summaries

## Source and triggers

- Daily report is generated by `production:send-daily-summary` from `ProductionSummaryService::forDate()`.
- Weekly report is generated by `production:send-weekly-summary` from `ProductionSummaryService::forWeek()`.
- Existing trigger schedule, configured role recipients, queue lane, retries, and after-commit behavior remain unchanged.
- Both reports contain internal operational production data. Delivery is limited to active users in configured roles who currently hold `production.work_orders.view`, with valid email addresses.

## Content

Daily values, quality counts, work-order rows, defects, and breakdown rows come directly from the summary service output. It shows at most ten work orders and five breakdowns, then reports the remaining row count and links to the production work-order page. User-controlled product and machine names are escaped by Blade; raw downtime descriptions are omitted.

Weekly totals and week-over-week change come from `forWeek()`. A null comparison is reported as unavailable because the prior week had no good units; no percentage is invented.

Subjects use configured email branding and exact report dates. Both messages use the shared light/dark branded shell, responsive tables, one authenticated SPA action, and generated plain-text alternatives. No report attachment is produced.

## Review and verification

HTML/text assertions cover source values, labels, long reports, escaping, summary truncation, route, and subject. Both Laravel notification MIME bodies were rendered through the `array` mail transport; no external email was sent. Chromium previews were reviewed at 1440px desktop and 390px mobile, in light and dark modes. Visual review found and fixed mobile overflow in the six-column work-order table by stacking each work order's details. Document width now matches viewport at every capture. Company contact/legal values are redacted in the saved artifacts.
