## Required separate fixes

- E-031–E-033: remove email delivery of temporary passwords. Use a one-time setup/reset flow; do not expose credentials in fallback alerts. E-030 has no direct caller found and should remain classified as dormant until proven otherwise.
- E-034: build the CRM inquiry CTA from the configured frontend base URL and resolve failure recipients through effective CRM permission logic.
- E-026–E-028: verify role settings against current effective route/API permissions before redesign; summaries contain operational production data.

## Limits

This pass did not inspect the production settings values, run scheduled commands, query recipient roles, inspect rendered output, or test queue/provider behavior. No statement here establishes external email-client rendering or legal compliance.
