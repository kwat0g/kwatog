## Recommendation event retention

Retention remains a proposal, not an approved policy. `dss:archive-events` manually archives recommendation events older than 12 months to the private local disk and retains source rows by default. Dry run previews eligible rows without writing. There is no scheduler entry. Physical deletion and non-12-month retention fail closed unless `DSS_EVENT_RETENTION_APPROVAL_REFERENCE` is configured with the retention owner's recorded approval reference. No such approval reference was available for this implementation. Archives are deterministic per calendar month and verified by row count and SHA-256 before any explicitly approved deletion; reason and outcome text are omitted from command output. Event reason and outcome-note storage is capped at 1,000 characters.
