### Current-run decision question

1. **Legacy permission slugs:** Scenario: an admin assigns only `crm.customers.manage`, `crm.so.create`, `quality.inspections.create`, or `quality.inspections.edit` to a custom role. Why unresolved: these slugs remain in the seeded permission catalog but have no runtime references; current routes use other slugs, and code does not define whether these are aliases or obsolete. Options: remove/migrate the slugs; map each legacy slug narrowly to its corresponding action; retain them as inert entries. Recommendation: confirm intended custom-role compatibility, then map only if backward compatibility is required; never expand create/edit into manage. Interim guard: current server checks exact active slugs and returns 403; no permission is silently broadened.
2. **Editing source-linked invoices:** Scenario: a delivery-generated invoice needs a correction while still draft. Why unresolved: invoice lines derive from confirmed delivery records, but the system does not define whether Finance may amend the invoice or must correct the source. Options: edit invoice lines and revalidate delivery totals; correct the delivery and regenerate the draft; prohibit edits and replace via credit note/reissue. Recommendation: correct the source before finalization, then use credit note/reissue after finalization. Interim guard: only manual prebill drafts are editable; source-linked standard drafts receive 422 and have no edit control.
3. **Invoice and prebill effective dates:** Scenario: Finance creates a future-dated or backdated invoice and posts it. Why unresolved: the roadmap says invoice timing policy still needs definition; no document sets a date window. Options: keep any valid date whose accounting period is not closed; restrict posting to the current/open period with explicit exceptions; require a separate reason and approval for dates outside the current period. Recommendation: keep the existing open-period gate until Finance sets the supported date window. Interim guard: date must parse, due date must not precede invoice date, and explicitly closed periods return 422; no extra date restriction was added. Observed: the real UI prebill chain posted a future-dated invoice; a Finance maker/checker/poster API walk posted a `2026-06-15` invoice and cancellation reversed its journal in the isolated DB.
4. **Manual prebill policy:** Scenario: Finance needs an invoice before confirmed delivery. Why unresolved: the roadmap says an approved prebill/pro-forma lifecycle should exist only if the business requires it, and does not say whether it may post AR and revenue before delivery. Options: keep the current reasoned three-actor posting flow; convert it to a non-posting pro-forma that converts after delivery; require confirmed delivery before any invoice. Recommendation: use a non-posting pro-forma if prebilling is required. Interim guard: existing prebill creation requires a reason, approval, and a different poster; standard invoices without a confirmed delivery are rejected. Existing behavior remains pending policy.

#### Decision questions

1. **Scheduler-stale critical email recipients.** Scenario: `scheduler_stale` raises a critical alert, but no role is configured for its email fan-out. The code records a failed attempt and retries; it cannot infer who owns scheduler health. Options: (a) add `system_admin` as default (recommended; system-wide issue, existing role), (b) route to production operations roles (more operational visibility, unclear infrastructure ownership), or (c) define a dedicated operations/on-call role (clear ownership, requires role provisioning and staffing rules). Recommendation: (a), with the existing admin setting allowing site override. Interim guard: keep the critical alert durable and retry state recorded; the alert remains available to users with `alerts.view`. No workflow advances based on email delivery. No default role added pending decision.
2. **Refund or retained balance for a customer return after full payment.** Scenario: a return is raised against a paid invoice and its credit note is finalized with no other open invoice. Why unresolved: the customer credit can be applied only to another finalized or partially paid invoice; there is no cash-refund path, and business policy does not say whether to refund or retain the balance. Options: (a) add a maker-checker cash refund with a posted journal and audit trail, (b) retain the unapplied credit for a later open invoice (recommended interim; credit-note balance already supports this), or (c) require an external Finance settlement process. Tradeoffs: (a) needs payment-method, approval, and reconciliation rules; (b) may delay cash repayment; (c) leaves settlement outside the system. Interim guard: paid invoices cannot receive another credit application; the UI says when no eligible target exists, and no refund/payment is issued.
3. **Manual vehicle status transitions.** Scenario: a fleet manager moves a vehicle directly among available, in_use, maintenance, and retired; observed behavior accepts all 16 pairs when no scheduled or active delivery references the vehicle. Why unresolved: delivery transitions own in_use/available changes, but fleet managers also have a manual status selector and no explicit transition policy exists for maintenance, retirement, or reactivation. Options: (a) keep any transition under fleet.manage (flexible; can create statuses that disagree with dispatch), (b) make in_use delivery-owned and allow maintenance/retirement/recommission transitions (stronger consistency; needs a clear recovery path), or (c) add an audited vehicle-service lifecycle with separate maintenance and retirement actions (best traceability; broader work). Recommendation: (b), with a narrow audited admin recovery action. Interim guard: only fleet.manage users can change status, and status changes/archive are rejected while any scheduled, loading, or in-transit delivery references the vehicle.
4. **Vehicle asset and maintenance association.** Scenario: fleet needs a capital asset link, depreciation, or maintenance work order. Why unresolved: schema/model contain `vehicles.asset_id`, but API/UI do not let users set it and maintenance excludes vehicles. Options: (a) keep fleet records operational-only (smallest scope; asset accounting stays separate), (b) add only an asset selector (links cost records but no vehicle maintenance workflow), or (c) integrate vehicles with asset accounting and maintenance orders (complete traceability; broader permissions and lifecycle). Recommendation: (c) if fleet units are capitalized and maintained in this system. Interim guard: the API ignores `asset_id`; no false association or vehicle maintenance action is presented.

Why unresolved: delegations stop working when the delegator is inactive, and no step-reassignment or escalation process exists. Options: (1) audited admin reassignment to another eligible actor (preserves chain, requires distinct actor and scope checks); (2) automatically advance to the next role (can skip required review); (3) cancel and resubmit (restarts work). Recommendation: option 1. Interim guard: inactive users and their delegates cannot approve; the record remains pending rather than advancing without authority.
- **DECISION QUESTION 14 — delegate consent and notice:** Scenario: a delegator creates cover while the delegate is unaware or unavailable. Why unresolved: delegation activates immediately and no acceptance or notification step exists. Options: (1) notify and activate immediately (simple, may assign unwanted duties); (2) require delegate acceptance (clear consent, cover may remain pending); (3) require acceptance only for sensitive roles (more policy, uneven behavior). Recommendation: option 2. Interim guard: delegated authority remains limited to the delegator's current role, active accounts, existing maker-checker rules, and module scope; consent and delivery status remain unverified.
- **DECISION QUESTION 15 — reusing a revoked delegation window:** Scenario: a user intentionally needs to grant the same person and role for the same dates after revocation. Why unresolved: current API treats an exact retry of a revoked grant as a stale retry and returns 422; no explicit reactivation action exists. Options: (1) keep revoked rows terminal and require a new date window (clearest history, may need a wider window); (2) add an audited Reactivate action with a reason (supports legitimate reuse, requires approval policy); (3) accept another POST as reactivation (ambiguous and unsafe).
