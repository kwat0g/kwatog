# Remaining business modules: deep audit and improvement recommendations

Date: 2026-10-05. Audit complete. Update 2026-10-06: **the seven priority fixes are implemented and verified**; the eighteen lower-priority findings remain planned. See [implementation results and evidence](major-integrity-hardening-20261006.md).

The current workspace was inspected, including its existing uncommitted changes. This audit covers Supply Chain, Maintenance, Accounting/Finance, Assets, and the related Purchasing, HR, Payroll, Budgeting, Forecasting, Returns, and administration connections. It supplements the [Warehouse audit](warehouse-deep-audit-20261005.md); it does not certify every calculation or historical record in the entire ERP.

The principal risks are incorrect financial presentation, duplicate money/stock intent after retries, equipment being released without repair, inconsistent backdated asset disposal, and shipment documents using order quantities rather than shipment quantities. Existing protections were checked before recording new findings. No product code, live business records, production queues, or live messages were changed during the audit. The subsequent implementation and additive schema migration are documented separately in the results linked above.

## Prioritized findings

There are **25 tracked findings: seven P1 and eighteen P2; no P0 established**. P1 here means materially incorrect money, stock, document, equipment, or authorization behavior. P2 includes narrower functional gaps, workflow risks, UX defects, and explicit policy questions. Confidence labels matter: a source-confirmed mechanism is not a reproduced production incident.

### Accounting and Assets

| ID | Priority / evidence | Trigger, impact, and smallest correction |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | P1, reproduced arithmetic | `BalanceSheetService.php:61` uses normal-balance orientation, then adds every asset positively. Account 1410 is a credit-normal contra asset. PPE 1000 plus depreciation 100 produces assets **1100**, equity **900**, and an unbalanced sheet. Calculate assets as debit minus credit; liabilities/equity as credit minus debit. Preserve trial-balance semantics and negative contra amounts. |
| F2 | P1, reproduced retry mechanism | `CreditNoteService.php:233` locks balances but `apply()` has no operation identity. Repeating one partial application of 100 against credit/invoice balances 1000 creates two applications and leaves both at **800**. This is a lost-response retry risk; two intentionally separate applications are valid. Add a stable per-intent key, unique constraint, and payload comparison. |
| F3 | P1, reproduced service and approval paths | `AssetService.php:338,520` permits disposal before the latest depreciation month. `DepreciationService.php:294` resets accumulated depreciation to existing historical rows without reversing subsequent posted history. January–September history followed by March disposal leaves April–September rows and their posted journal identity while the register rewinds. Reject unsupported backdating at request and execution under lock. A correction workflow is separate work. |
| F4 | P2, reproduced create request | `StoreAssetRequest.php:64` and `UpdateAssetRequest.php:57` compare money in after-validation callbacks even after decimal validation fails. `is_numeric('1e3')` succeeds, but BCMath rejects it: asset creation returns **500**. Skip comparisons when either field has errors and validate scalar decimal values before casting. Add update/array-input cases; those variants were source-reviewed, not all runtime-probed. Acquisition/insured money also lacks a request maximum matching `decimal(15,2)`; bound it at `9999999999999.99` to prevent valid-looking oversized decimals reaching a database overflow. This additional domain gap is source-confirmed. |
| F5 | P2, source-confirmed | Insurance fields are accepted on create and exposed by `AssetResource`, but omitted from update request/service and asset edit/detail UI. Renewals cannot be maintained through the shipped flow. Display the existing fields and allow guarded updates without reopening acquisition cost or depreciation history. |
| F6 | P2, source-confirmed policy mismatch | Zero-cost acquisition is allowed, but zero-cost/no-proceeds disposal tells users to correct acquisition cost, which the update whitelist prevents. Choose a coherent policy. Recommended: an approved register-only disposal for genuinely zero-cost assets, with no fabricated journal and an explicit audit record. Approval is required before changing this accounting behavior. |
| F7 | P2, reproduced policy dependency | Balance-sheet assets are all-time, but synthetic income is current fiscal year only. A prior-year cash sale of 100, with no closing journal, gives next-year assets **100** and equity **0**. `FiscalYearService::close()` only changes status. This does not establish missing closing journals in live books. Define mandatory closing/reconciliation checks before considering any year-end automation; do not automatically post retained earnings. |

The F1/F7 arithmetic fixtures create balanced posted journals directly to isolate reporting; they are not tests of journal-posting authorization. The F3 approval fixture uses existing posted journal identity for depreciation rows, including a synthetic aggregate journal, rather than recreating nine monthly posting jobs. It establishes the disposal/history inconsistency, not a complete production ledger reconciliation.

### Maintenance and equipment connections

| ID | Priority / evidence | Trigger, impact, and smallest correction |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | P2, reproduced | Updating only a description postpones an unperformed calendar PM because `MaintenanceScheduleService.php:292` anchors it to `now()`. A 30-day schedule created October 5 moves from November 4 to November 5 after an October 6 spelling correction. Preserve the original anchor/due date for non-scheduling edits. Only explicit interval/anchor changes may alter it. |
| M2 | P2, reproduced stale-hint mechanism | `MaintenanceWorkOrderService::create()` accepts a passed schedule after it becomes inactive. A stale due-list item can still materialize a maintenance WO. Reload and lock the schedule, recheck active state, current due/threshold eligibility, and existing open WO. The normal scanner already filters active schedules; the missing check is at materialization. |
| M3 | P1, reproduced | Starting maintenance on a Breakdown machine and canceling with “repair not performed” changes it to **Idle**. Cancellation closes downtime/restores equipment without proof that it is serviceable. Preserve the pre-maintenance fault or unresolved fault condition. Completion with valid repair evidence may release it, subject to existing ownership/sibling guards. |
| M4 | P2, reproduced plus policy question | Assignment accepts any active HR employee, and changing an already assigned technician hits `Assigned to Assigned` transition failure. The absent handover is a concrete gap. Technician qualification/department/account eligibility needs a documented policy; membership alone does not prove competence. Add a small authorized change-assignee action with old/new history and fresh eligibility checks. Do not expand ordinary employee permissions. |
| M5 | P1, reproduced retry mechanism | `SparePartUsageService::record()` has no stable issue identity. Replaying one quantity-2 issue creates two usages and reduces stock 10 to **6**, with duplicate cost. Add operation-key replay protection across usage, movements, and WO cost in one transaction. Distinct legitimate issues must remain possible. |
| M6 | P2, reproduced | Maintenance issues use one `StockMovementService::move()` call. A request for 8 from two usable lots of 5 fails against the first lot despite sufficient total stock. The shared `issueAcrossLots()` already exists. Reuse it and retain each movement/lot identity with the usage; preserve QC, expiry, stock-count, UoM, and reservation checks. |
| M7 | P2, reproduced role contract | A role with `maintenance.view` and `maintenance.schedules.manage` can open schedule options but cannot fetch machines or maintenance target options. The schedule form uses MRP endpoints; the narrow maintenance lookup requires WO-create permission. Supply schedule management with an authorized minimal equipment lookup. Do not grant full MRP access to solve a lookup problem. |
| M8 | P2, source-confirmed UX/performance gap | Hours/shots schedules expose little due-progress context; calendar-style due cells show “—” for threshold schedules. Resource lookups also fetch individual targets. Show current hours/shots, baseline, threshold, remaining amount, active state, and existing open maintenance WO; batch target loading. Daily threshold generation already exists. |

An additional stale-description/completion date regression was investigated and **not reproduced**. The existing monotonic completion guard remained effective in the probe; it is excluded from findings.

### Supply Chain and Purchasing

| ID | Priority / evidence | Trigger, impact, and smallest correction |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | P1, reproduced renderer inputs | `ImpexDocumentService.php:45,69` supplies every PO line at its full ordered quantity to packing-list/commercial-invoice rendering. A shipment/GRN receiving **10** against PO **100** still supplies **100**. Model a shipment-specific manifest or reconcile approved receipt lines for received shipments. Until actual contents are established, generated PO-intent documents must be clearly drafts. Do not overwrite supplier commercial invoices or treat accepted quantity as the shipped quantity. |
| S2 | P2, reproduced | Container update accepts `container_number: null`, then fails the database NOT NULL constraint with **500**. Require a nonblank identifier when supplied; omitted identifier should preserve the existing value. Audit required enum fields against the same nullable pattern. |
| S3 | P2, reproduced | Container gross weight **10** and net weight **20** are accepted. Existing numeric precision validation does not enforce the physical relationship. Compare effective persisted-plus-patch values and return field-specific 422 when net exceeds gross. Do not require unknown weights prematurely. |
| S4 | P2, source-confirmed concurrency hazard | `ContainerService.php:54,66,76` locks child then Shipment; `ShipmentService::updateMeta()` locks Shipment then Containers. Concurrent edits can invert locks. Standardize parent-first acquisition and recheck child ownership/state under lock. A real deadlock was not generated during this audit. |
| V1 | P2, source-confirmed misleading metric | `SupplierPerformanceService.php:444` calculates “price variance” from **quantity receipt shortfall**, not purchase/receipt prices; the supplier UI labels it Price variance. A pending partial receipt can look like a price problem and affect the price component of the supplier score. Use actual comparable price evidence, otherwise show unavailable and omit that component from normalized scoring. Preserve any receipt-shortfall metric under its correct name. |

S1 tests mock the renderer/vault and inspect renderer payloads. They do not inspect a final printed PDF. S4 is an observed code-order hazard, not an allegation that a production deadlock occurred.

### Cross-module reliability and access

## Automation recommendations

Reuse current outbox, notifications, scheduler, run status, locks, and operation identities. Automate preparation and reminders; preserve physical proof, accounting approval, and exception decisions.

| Candidate | Trigger / owner | Automatic work | Safeguards / human decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1: reliable PM generation | Calendar or actual hour/shot threshold; Maintenance | Create one eligible open maintenance WO and show its source/due reason | Fix M1/M2 first; lock/recheck schedule and target, existing open-WO identity, stable completion anchor. Never mark equipment repaired merely because job canceled. |
| A2: maintenance technician handoff | Assignment/reassignment; Maintenance supervisor | Suggest qualified eligible technicians; notify current assignee and log handover | Fix M4; preserve access policy, approval/account eligibility and absence checks where applicable. Assignment suggestion is not a grant of broad permissions. |
| A3: material issue preparation | Confirmed maintenance issue intent; Warehouse/Maintenance | Suggest usable lots, split the authorized quantity and calculate cost | Fix M5/M6; one issue identity, real physical issue confirmation, shared stock gates. Do not auto-consume merely from a WO plan. |
| A4: shipment reconciliation | Manifest, shipment receipt or revised documents; Impex | Compare PO intent, manifested quantity and received quantity; prepare correct document drafts and mismatch worklist | Fix S1 first. Use actual supplier/manifest evidence. Customs clearance and commercial invoice authority remain reviewed. |
| A5: trusted supplier score refresh | Accepted receipts/QC/cost evidence changes; Purchasing | Recompute affected supplier-period snapshot using existing command/outbox machinery | Fix mislabeled V1 metric first; dedupe vendor-period events, distinguish missing evidence from zero variance, keep score components inspectable. |
| A6: reliable return alerts | Customer return created; Returns/Warehouse | Deliver a deduplicated next-action alert or show blocked delivery for retry | Fix X2; durable event and recipient resolution; do not auto-approve returns or physically restock goods. |
| A7: staffing disruption review | Approved leave, shift/calendar change, employee deactivation; PPC | Flag affected upcoming WOs and propose eligible replacement/rescheduling | Start/confirmation checks already block unsafe eligibility. Add proactive visibility using current assignment tables; debounce/coalesce by affected WO. A running WO requires explicit handover. |
| A8: finance exception worklists | Accepted GRN, confirmed delivery, failed source job, pending payment/depreciation | Link receipts lacking reviewed bill drafts, deliveries awaiting invoice review, blocked dunning and failed accounting handoffs | Reuse existing durable source/run identities and approval queue. No automatic posting/payment approval or duplicate dashboards. |
| A9: asset reconciliation and renewal reminders | Monthly close and insurance expiry; Finance/Assets | Show register-versus-GL discrepancies and policy renewal work | Fix F1/F3/F5; reconcile acquisition/contra balances and later depreciation after disposal. Never auto-correct historical journals. |
| A10: safe export lifecycle | Owner disablement/permission change or scheduled run; Admin/data owner | Pause unauthorized schedules and show reason; validate queued delivery again | Fix X1. Fresh owner/column/row scope, stop revoked queued sends, preserve retry status and retention policy. |
| A11: trusted shortage suggestions | Forecast/stock/QC/expiry change; PPC/Purchasing | Recompute advisory usable availability and prepare shortage review | Fix X3; reuse warehouse availability, disclose source freshness, never auto-place PO from an unverified advisory result. |

Already present: daily calendar/threshold PM scanning, accepted-GRN bill preparation, delivery-to-finance notifications, AR dunning, monthly depreciation requests, budget-actual sync, training-expiry reminders, supplier-performance recomputation, export leases, and reopened-period relocking. Improve these existing paths rather than adding duplicate schedulers.

“Return handover” and “Return unused” remain physical attestations. The system can prepare remaining quantities, identify the source WO/delivery, suggest locations/lots, and notify the receiving party. It must not assume material is physically back in the warehouse, convert a production reject into reusable stock, or free a truck before receipt confirmation.

## Recommended sequence and stop condition

1. Fix P1 integrity risks: F1, F2, F3, M3, M5, S1, X1. Existing immutable-history and approval protections stay.
2. Fix narrow request, lookup, calendar, lot, lock-order and advisory gaps. Resolve F6/F7 and technician qualification policies before dependent behavior changes.
3. Polish the measured layouts and due/next-action context within current design tokens.
4. Add the bounded, deduplicated automation above and test both success and blocked/retry paths.
