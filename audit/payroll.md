# Audit: payroll — 2026-09-06

## Summary

The payroll unit is the most hardened module audited so far: the double-pay guard is genuinely race-proof (DB-unique `payroll_cycle_claims` keyed on a derived cycle key, read-then-insert with the unique index as the real fence), the compute claim uses token-fenced conditional UPDATEs with stale takeover, and void/approve/finalize all lock-then-guard inside transactions. Money math is bcmath string-based end to end; resources keep the decimal-as-string contract; the bank file reconciles its own total against the period before writing. The real defects cluster in the **13th-month path**, which bypasses several guards the normal path enforces: its GL posting drops the BIR correction withholding (PY-01), its net pay is unclamped (PY-03), its payroll_date is unbounded (PY-05), and its tax reconciliation window is status-sensitive (PY-08). Second cluster: gov deductions silently zero when no contribution table exists (PY-02), and the anomaly gate that should backstop that can itself be silently disabled (PY-10) or bypassed via recompute (PY-04). Net-pay clamping writes off unrecovered deductions instead of carrying them forward (PY-06). The SPA is solid (states, string decimals, HashIDs, confirmation dialogs with stakes); findings there are a dead search box and two guard/message mismatches. 40 feature-test files cover the core lifecycle well; the gaps named below are untested.

### PY-01 — 13th-month GL posting drops the withheld BIR correction (High, Missing)

`PayrollGlPostingService::postLocked()` builds the 13th-month JE as `debit thirteenth_expense $net; credit thirteenth_payable $net` (PayrollGlPostingService.php:251-256), where `$net = SUM(net_pay) = SUM(gross − correctionDelta)`. When the annual reconciliation withholds tax (`thirteenth_month_correction_delta > 0`, stored as `withholding_tax` on the 13th-month payroll rows — ThirteenthMonthService.php:301-307), that withheld amount belongs to BIR as a withholding-tax liability, but no line credits `withholding_payable`. The entry still balances (debit net = credit net), so the balance assertion at line 308 passes, and the books understate both 13th-month expense and the tax liability by exactly the correction total — the remittance will later leave the bank with no liability on the ledger to clear. The normal branch handles the analogous case correctly (it credits withholding_payable and derives the floor adjustment). Fix: debit expense with gross, credit withholding_payable with `SUM(thirteenth_month_correction_delta)`, credit thirteenth_payable with net. There is **no test coverage** of 13th-month GL posting at all (PayrollGlPostingTest only exercises `is_thirteenth_month => false`), and ThirteenthMonthTest asserts the zero-deduction case ("net_pay must equal gross_pay", ThirteenthMonthTest.php:197-198) while the replacement-run test at lines 150-162 proves correctionDelta can be ₱130,000.

### PY-02 — Missing gov tables silently compute zero deductions (High, Risk)

All four gov computation services treat "no brackets found" as "contribution is ₱0.00": SssComputationService.php:46-55 (empty collection → last() null → 0.00), BirTaxComputationService.php:56-57, PagibigComputationService.php:60, PhilhealthComputationService.php:39-41. `bracketsEffectiveOn()` (GovernmentContributionTableService.php:49-76) falls back from dated rows to the active set, and from there to an empty collection — so a fresh install, a deactivated table, or a payroll_date beyond the newest effective table yields a fully computed, approvable, finalizable period in which **nobody** had SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG/BIR withheld. The calculator's own comment block (PayrollCalculatorService.php:219-238) treats the contribution basis as load-bearing statutory money, and the de minimis lookup deliberately hard-blocks on failure (lines 785-804) — the asymmetry is the defect. The only backstop is `large_change` anomaly flags versus the prior period, which (a) don't exist for first-payroll employees and (b) depend on a detector that is itself best-effort (PY-10). Fix: make an empty bracket set a BusinessRuleException per employee (error row blocks approve, same as de minimis), or a precompute check that blocks claimForCompute when any agency has no table effective on payroll_date.

## Cross-module flags

- **attendance** — Night-differential basis: `aggregateAttendance()` pays ND as `ndHrs × hourly × 0.10` with **no `day_type_rate` multiplier** (PayrollCalculatorService.php:472-475), while OT on the same rows does multiply by rate (line 469). If the DTR engine records `night_diff_hours` on premium days (holiday/rest-day night shifts), ND is underpaid by the day-rate factor. Verify DTR semantics and either multiply ND by `rate` or document that nd-hours are ordinary-day-only. Also confirm OT min-30min/max-4h and extended-shift auto-OT are enforced DTR-side (not visible from payroll).
- **hr** — CLAUDE.md's payroll law names `Employee::employedDayFraction()` as the proration authority; that method does **not** exist on Employee — proration is a private method of PayrollCalculatorService (PayrollCalculatorService.php:559-587). HR's `FinalPayService::lastSalaryProRated()` consumes `payroll.basic_pay` (correct), but hire/separation proration logic exists in two codebases (payroll calculator vs HR final-pay/clearance paths); a change to one cannot be seen by the other. Consider hoisting the fraction onto Employee as the docs promise.
- **accounting** — PY-01 lands wrong balances on Accounting's books (expense + withholding liability understated for 13th-month runs); the JE reversal path used by void() (JournalEntryService::reverse) was read-only reviewed and looks sound.
- **spa/shared** — PY-15's explicit multipart Content-Type also appears in spa/src/api/attendance/attendances.ts:54, spa/src/api/hr/employees.ts:111, spa/src/api/hr/employee-documents.ts:12 — convention violation is cross-cutting (currently neutralised by axios 1.18; a downgrade or adapter change re-breaks uploads).
