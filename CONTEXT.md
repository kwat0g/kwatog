# Payroll

## Terms

- **Payroll maker:** The HR Officer who creates, computes, and corrects a payroll period.
- **Payroll checker:** The Finance Officer who reviews a computed payroll and either approves it or requests a correction.
- **Correction request:** A Finance decision that returns a computed payroll to Draft with a required reason. It is not a rejection of employee pay; it is a request for HR to correct and recompute the period.
- **Finalizer/disburser:** The Finance Officer who finalizes an approved period, produces the bank file, records disbursement, and attaches bank evidence.

## Lifecycle

`Draft → Processing → Computed → Approved → Finalized → Disbursed`

A correction request transitions `Computed → Draft`; HR recomputes it and Finance reviews it again. A finalized period is not corrected through this path.
