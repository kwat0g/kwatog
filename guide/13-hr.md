# HR — Employees, Departments, Positions, Separation & Clearance

Sign in first at `/sign-in` (password for all demo accounts: `password`).
HR screens need HR permissions — use `hr@ogami.test` (Maria Santos,
HR officer). The plain `employee@ogami.test` CANNOT open these pages.

## 13.1 Employees (`/hr/employees`)

1. Open `/hr/employees`. Title: **Employees**.
2. Search box reads **"Search name or employee no…"** —
   try `DEMO-0001` (a seeded demo employee number).
3. To add someone: click **Add employee**.
4. The form page (`/hr/employees/create`) is titled **Add employee**.
   Fill in the fields and save.
5. Click any row to open the detail page (`/hr/employees/:id`).
6. On the detail page you can edit, and start a separation (see 13.4).
7. Salary adjustments live as a TAB on `/hr/employees`
   (there is no separate salary-adjustments page).
8. Permissions: view needs `hr.employees.view`;
   create needs `hr.employees.create`; edit needs `hr.employees.edit`.
   Sensitive data (SSS, TIN, bank) additionally needs
   `hr.employees.view_sensitive` — other roles see masked values.

## 13.2 Departments (`/hr/departments`)

1. Open `/hr/departments`. Title: **Departments**,
   subtitle shows the count.
2. The page shows an **Organisation tree** panel plus a
   **Details** panel when you select a department.
3. To add: open the department dialog — its title reads
   **Add department** (editing reads **Edit department**).
4. To remove: the dialog asks **Delete department?** with a
   **Delete** button. Staff still assigned there may block it.
5. Seeded departments include: Executive (EXEC), Human Resources (HR),
   Finance & Accounting (FIN), Production (PROD), Quality Control (QC),
   Warehouse & Logistics (WH), Purchasing & Procurement (PUR),
   Production Planning (PPC), Maintenance & Engineering (MAINT),
   Mold Department (MOLD), Import/Export (IMPEX),
   Sales & Marketing (SALES), Customer Service (CS),
   Admin & General Affairs (ADMIN), IT (IT).
6. Permission: `hr.departments.view` to open,
   `hr.departments.manage` to add/edit/delete (HR officer holds both).

## 13.3 Positions (`/hr/positions`)

1. Open `/hr/positions`. Title: **Positions** with a count subtitle.
2. Search box reads **"Search title…"**.
3. To add: the dialog title reads **Add position**
   (editing reads **Edit position**). Each position belongs
   to one department.
4. To remove: confirm **Delete position?** with **Delete**.
   Employees still in that position may block it.
5. Permission: `hr.positions.view` to open,
   `hr.positions.manage` to change (HR officer holds both).

## 13.4 Separation & clearance (`/hr/separations`)

1. Start FROM the employee: open `/hr/employees`, click the leaver,
   and choose the separation action on their detail page.
   On success you see: "Separation initiated. Complete clearance
   and final pay before the employee status changes."
2. Track it at `/hr/separations`. Title: **Separations & clearances**.
   A demo clearance `CLR-DEMO-001` is seeded for clicking through.
3. Open one row (`/hr/separations/:id`). The progress strip reads:
   Initiated → (department signs) → Finalized.
4. Each owner signs ONLY their department's items with the **Sign**
   button:
   - Step 1 — Department head (`depthead@ogami.test`) signs own-team items.
   - Step 2 — Warehouse (`warehouse@ogami.test`) signs materials-return.
   - Step 3 — Maintenance (`maintenance@ogami.test`) signs pending-work.
   - Step 4 — Finance (`finance@ogami.test`) signs cash-advance/loan items.
5. HR finishes with the **Finalize** button
   (dialog title **Finalize separation?**, confirm **Finalize**).
   Only then does the employee status change and final pay compute.
6. Started by mistake? HR uses the cancel path — verify exact button in app.
7. Permissions: `hr.separation.view` to see the list,
   `hr.separation.initiate` to start, `hr.clearance.sign`
   to sign, `hr.separation.finalize` to close.
